# Optional practice interview

This is a separately authorized, human-operated synthetic practice and acceptance exercise, not an installation prerequisite. It is also the decisive next check when **Test Recall & ngrok** warns that this Mac could not verify its local-origin public route: Recall-originated delivery follows a different network path. First finish [Save and choose a workspace](../README.md#save-and-choose-a-workspace), or return to the app if valid settings are already saved; stop on any Save/runtime failure. Keep normal network and security policy. Use no customer data. The retained step labels match historical acceptance evidence.

### 18b. Create a practice prep

**Interview Mac, text editor:** write a [Markdown interview prep](prep-format.md). For a synthetic starter, copy **prep/TEMPLATE.md** into **prep/current** and save the copy as `practice.md`. Edit its title and bullet questions in an ordinary text editor; keep the template for reuse.

Expected: `prep/current/practice.md` is readable Markdown. Next: 18c.

### 18c. Choose the practice prep

**Interview Mac, Caddy → Workspace and prep:** click **Choose prep…**. The native macOS Open panel starts in **workspace/prep**; open **current**, select `practice.md`, and click **Open**. Its sidebar and folder navigation can also select a Markdown prep elsewhere, including a template or archived prep. Caddy takes a uniquely named working copy in **prep/current** and binds the selected original for pre-conversation saves. Before capture starts, Save and automatic saves update both files; after capture starts, edits stay in the session and finished export.

Expected: the prep title, duration and Must/More Avenues prompts populate the app. Cancel leaves the current selection intact; invalid files show an error. Prep cannot change after capture starts. Next: 19a.

### 19a. Get a meeting link

**Interview Mac, Meet or Teams:** create and join a private Google Meet or personal Teams meeting alone, then copy its meeting link (`https://meet.google.com/abc-defg-hij` or `https://teams.live.com/meet/` followed by digits). Alone is fine for this synthetic route check. A Recall bot counts as a participant, so Google Meet's free three-participant/60-minute limit applies to interviewer, guest, and bot. Native Google transcription is not required.

Expected: you are in that meeting and have its link on the clipboard. Use no real customer data. Next: 19b.

### 19b. Authorize starting capture

**Interview Mac, Caddy:** choose **Google Meet** or **Microsoft Teams (personal)** to match the meeting, then paste its link. Only if you authorize Recall capture and its possible provider cost/retention, click **Start live capture** once. Do not create another bot if a later stage fails.

Expected: the visible **Convo Caddy** bot waits for admission in the selected meeting. If it does not, stop acceptance and report the visible state. Next: 19c.

### 19c. Admit the recording bot

**Interview Mac, Meet or Teams:** admit the bot only when everyone present agrees to recording/transcription. **Admission is recording authorization.**

Expected: its ten-second recording/transcription notice appears. If not, stop the practice and report what happened. Next: 19d.

### 19d. Check the transcript

**Interview Mac:** speak a synthetic sentence in the meeting, then deliberately open Caddy's transcript.

Expected: the actual sentence appears as live transcript text in Caddy, not merely a bot-joined state. Setup checks cannot prove this live result; if it fails, preserve whether failure occurred at creation, lobby, admission, transcript, or finalization, then stop rather than repeatedly creating bots or disabling protections. Next: 20a.

### 20a. Check a prepared question

**Interview Mac, Caddy:** check the starter question under **Prepared Questions**.

Expected: the checked item fades and stays visible. Next: 20b.

### 20b. Add a practice note

**Interview Mac, Caddy's main textbox:** enter `/note practice` and press Return.

Expected: a literal note appears with no model call. Next: 20c.

### 20c. End the meeting

**Interview Mac, Meet or Teams:** end the practice meeting for everyone or remove the bot.

Expected: confirm the bot departs and Caddy starts finalizing the conversation. Quitting Convo Caddy does not remove a meeting bot; check the meeting if it remains. Next: 20d.

### 20d. Wait for saving

**Interview Mac, Caddy:** wait for the finished conversation to appear in the completed list.

Expected: Caddy finalization completes and its four-file record and archived prep exist in the workspace. If saving reports a failure, retain the visible error and do not claim completion. Next: 20e.

### 20e. Reopen the app

**Interview Mac, Caddy:** quit normally, then reopen the verified copy from Applications.

Expected: the finished conversation remains listed. Finished: record what actually passed. Source tests do not guarantee live service success.
